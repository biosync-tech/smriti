Featured · Translational

Uncovering drug mechanism-of-action in tumor models

Lead compound worked in xenografts but the MoA was unclear — blocking IND-enabling work.

Problem: Efficacy signal without mechanism — regulatory narrative was stalled until the team could explain why it worked.

Approach: QC & alignment → differential expression (treated vs. control) → pathway enrichment → upstream regulator analysis → cross-reference with public drug-response databases.

Outcome: Surfaced two converging signaling pathways and a candidate resistance marker — anchored the MoA story for regulatory filings.

Featured · Immuno-oncology

Building a single-cell atlas of the tumor immune microenvironment

47 patient samples, no unified view of which immune states drove response vs. resistance.

Problem: Data existed but fragmented across batches; no cell-state framework to support patient stratification.

Approach: Harmonized QC across batches → integration & clustering → cell-type annotation → responder vs. non-responder differential testing → trajectory analysis on T-cell exhaustion.

Outcome: Two previously unappreciated exhaustion-associated states identified — now anchor the lead program's patient stratification strategy.

Featured · Digital health

Early sepsis prediction in the ICU

Investor diligence needed a defensible ICU early-warning baseline before clinical pilots.

Problem: Team had a model idea but no rigorous benchmark to stand behind in conversations with hospitals and investors.

Approach: MIMIC-III cohort extraction → feature engineering on vitals, labs & meds → time-aware splits → benchmarking against Sepsis-3 → interpretability via SHAP.

Outcome: Validated predictive model with AUROC > 0.85 at 6 hours pre-onset — de-risked diligence and anchored the first hospital pilot.

Featured · Drug discovery

A biomedical knowledge graph for drug-repurposing hypothesis generation

Scanning literature and public databases for an orphan disease — at a scale humans can't.

Problem: Manual literature review was unfeasible; scattered sources made systematic hypothesis generation impossible.

Approach: Built a KG linking genes, diseases, drugs, pathways & literature → entity resolution & ontology harmonization → graph embeddings → path-based scoring of drug-disease hypotheses → LLM-assisted evidence summarization.

Outcome: Ranked list of 14 candidate molecules with mechanistic rationale — two progressed into in-vitro validation.

What scientific leads usually ask first

What does a first engagement look like?

A 2–6 week fixed-scope sprint with an explicit deliverable (report, pipeline, figures, or diligence memo), success criteria, and a kill switch. NDA first. No long-term retainer required to start. Fixed-scope sprints typically range $15K–$75K depending on complexity and timeline.

Do you handle PHI / HIPAA-regulated data?

Yes, under a signed BAA, typically inside your HIPAA-eligible cloud tenancy. We are HIPAA-aligned and BAA-ready — not HIPAA certified. We do not take custody of regulated data unless the workflow requires it, and we will not accept PHI through the public form or ordinary email.

Who actually does the work?

One senior scientific owner, end to end. Specialists, if needed, are named under NDA. No bait-and-switch between the first call and delivery.

How long does a typical engagement take?

2–6 weeks for fixed-scope sprints. Straightforward analyses can ship in a couple of weeks; multi-modal work takes longer. Longer engagements shift to monthly retainers.
